Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data ReactionFlow ("we", "the Service") collects, why, and how it is used when you connect your TikTok account to publish AI-generated reaction videos.

1. Data we access via TikTok

When you authorize ReactionFlow through TikTok's OAuth login, we may access and process the following, limited strictly to the scopes you approve:

DataPurpose
Basic profile info (username, avatar, open ID)Identify your connected account and display it in the app's authorization flow, as required by TikTok.
Access & refresh tokensAuthenticate API requests made on your behalf (e.g., publishing a video). Stored securely and never shared with third parties.
video.publish / video.upload permissionPublish or draft the generated reaction videos to your TikTok account.

2. Data we generate or process

  • Source video clips selected for reaction content.
  • AI-generated avatar video and script used to build the reaction.
  • Final composed video file, temporarily stored until publishing completes.

3. How we use this data

Data is used exclusively to operate the Service: generating reaction content and publishing it to the TikTok account you connected. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use TikTok data to build advertising profiles.

4. Data retention

Access and refresh tokens are retained only while your account remains connected to the Service. Source and generated video files are retained only as long as needed to complete processing and publishing, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

5. Data sharing

We do not share your TikTok data with third parties, except where required to operate core functionality (e.g., cloud infrastructure providers acting strictly as data processors) or where required by law.

6. Your controls

  • You can revoke ReactionFlow's access to your TikTok account at any time from TikTok's app permissions settings.
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us (see below).

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored tokens and content, including restricted access and encrypted storage where applicable.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children under the age required by TikTok's own Terms of Service.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact 10coments@gmail.com.
